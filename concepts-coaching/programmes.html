Programmes

What each batch covers.

Three programmes, one syllabus. Each batch follows the school's chapters and adds a weekly test, so a parent can see progress without asking.

Class 9

Maths + Science

Number systems, polynomials, coordinate geometry, triangles, and the science chapters taught in school, in the same order.

  • Two classes a week
  • One chapter test a week
  • Doubt slot before each test
  • Notebook check every fortnight
Class 10

Board focus

Board pattern from October: full papers, marking-scheme practice and answer-writing drills.

  • Two classes a week
  • Board pattern papers
  • Revision rounds from November
  • Extra classes before exams
Doubt support

One-to-one

For a student stuck on one chapter, or one who missed a week of class.

  • 30-minute slots
  • Any chapter
  • Small groups or single
  • Booked on WhatsApp
