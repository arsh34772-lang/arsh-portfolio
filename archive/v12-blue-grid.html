02 — Why it happens

Six minutes. Nobody tells you.

The same sale, twice. One shop answers at eleven at night with nobody awake. The other finds out in the morning that it never happened.

11:04pm · last night
  1. 11:04 pm

    A man in Model Town searches “kitchen tiles near me”. Sofa, phone, television on in the background.

  2. 11:06 pm

    He taps your listing. A phone number, four photographs from 2019, and no rates. He cannot tell whether you stock the tile he wants.

  3. 11:08 pm

    All he needs is a size and a price. The page answers neither. He goes back and opens the next shop in the list.

  4. 11:10 pm

    The shop two streets down has a page that answers in three seconds, with a WhatsApp button that already says his tile's name in it.

  5. 11:12 pm

    He sends the message. Their phone buzzes on the counter. Nobody is awake to read it, and it still counts as a customer.

  6. 9:00 am

    They open with an order waiting. You open with a normal morning, one customer lighter, and no way to know it ever happened.

05 — Questions

The five things everyone asks.

If yours isn't here, send it on WhatsApp. You will get a straight answer, not a brochure.

Will the page belong to me, or to you?

To you. The domain and hosting are bought in your name with your own email, in front of you. When we finish, you change every password, turn on two-factor, and my access is removed. You get a signed one-page handover and a checklist.

What if I don't like what you build?

You see a real concept before you pay anything. If it isn't right, you walk away and owe nothing. Once you say yes, one round of changes is included, and most of those are wording and photographs.

Do you take the photographs?

I use what you already have — your phone photographs, your stock, your catalogue — and I crop and correct them so they look right on the page. I don't run a photo shoot, and I don't use AI images of your shop.

How do I pay, and when?

Half when we start, half when it goes live. UPI or bank transfer, in your own name on the invoice. No card details ever go on the website itself.

What happens after the week is over?

The page keeps running whether or not you take the ₹999 care plan. Care only covers changes, backups and small edits. Nothing switches off if you stop paying.
