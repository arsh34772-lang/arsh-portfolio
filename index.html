03 / About

The person doing the work.

I am Arshdeep, a graphic designer and front-end builder from Ludhiana. I started with posters and social content, then kept getting asked the same question: "can you make the website too?" So I learned to build it.

Every project starts with the customer holding a cheap phone on 4G. If the page is slow there, nothing else matters. I design for that phone first, then let the desktop look after itself.

I work in writing, show the real thing early, and put every account in your name at handover. No retainers you cannot leave.
