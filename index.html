05 — Questions

The five things every owner asks.

Straight answers, before you spend anything.

Will the page be mine, or yours?

Yours. Domain, hosting and business email are bought in your name with your card from day one. On handover you change every password, turn on two-factor, and my access is removed. You get a signed one-page agreement and a ticked checklist as proof.

What if I don't like it?

You see the first screen before I build the rest, so you never get a surprise at the end. One full round of changes is included. If it is wrong at that point, I stop and you don't pay the second half.

Do you take the photographs?

No. I use what you already have on your phone, your Instagram, or your old site, and I crop and grade them. If a photo is genuinely unusable I will tell you which three to reshoot, with the framing.

How do I pay?

Half to start, half on handover. Bank transfer or UPI. Your customers never enter card details on the site — orders go to WhatsApp, and any online payment runs through a gateway in your own name.

What happens after handover?

Nothing unless you want it. The page is yours and it keeps working. If you want small edits, backups and updates handled, that is the ₹999 care plan — and you can stop it any month.
