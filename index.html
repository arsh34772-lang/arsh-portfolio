01 — The day

You are busy all day and still losing sales you never hear about.

You stand at the counter from ten to nine. The phone keeps going. "Rate kya hai?" "Size hai?" "Delivery hoti hai?" "Photo bhej do." You answer every single one by hand, between customers, because nobody else can.

Then, late at night, one of them wants to buy. He types your shop's name into Google with his thumb. What does he get? Three old pictures on a listing. Or a website that takes twelve seconds to open on 4G and pushes the rate card into a PDF nobody opens.

He closes the tab and messages the shop down the road, the one with a proper page. You never find out. There is no missed call, no notification. Just a sale that quietly didn't happen, and a day that felt twice as long.
