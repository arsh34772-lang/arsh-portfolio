Before you visit

Questions we get every week

Do you supply and fit, or only sell?

Both. We supply the fittings and our own fitters install them, so there is one number to call if anything leaks or drips. If you already have a contractor, we supply only.

Can I see the fittings working before I buy?

Yes. Come to the Pakhowal Road showroom and see the fittings in person before you buy. Ask to see the finishes side by side.

What is the price range for a full bathroom?

It depends on tile size and fittings. A straightforward bathroom with Indian-brand fittings sits in one range, a Gessi or Keuco specification sits much higher. Send your plan on WhatsApp and you get a written range the same day.

Do you deliver outside Ludhiana?

Yes, across Punjab. Delivery is quoted separately by distance.
