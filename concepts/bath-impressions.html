The three questions we get daily

What does a full bathroom cost?

It depends on the four big pieces: WC, basin, shower and tiles. Send the bathroom size and your budget, and the list comes back with brands and prices that fit instead of a vague range.

Why is the same WC cheaper online?

Sometimes it is, sometimes it is a different variant or an older series. Send us the model you are comparing and we tell you which it is, and what our price is for the same piece.

Do you supply tiles along with the fittings?

Yes — Somany, Seron and others are on the floor, so the tile and the fitting are chosen together and arrive together.
