Stone by room

The right stone is a room decision, not a catalogue decision.

A polished marble that looks perfect in a drawing room will show every wet footmark in a bathroom. This is how we separate them.

01

Drawing room & stairs

Statuario, Crema and grey onyx in polished finish. Bigger slabs mean fewer joints, and fewer joints mean the floor reads as one piece.

02

Bathroom & wet areas

Honed travertine and matt granite. Grippy underfoot, and they don't show water marks the way a gloss slab does.

03

Kitchen platform

Black Marquina and coffee brown granite. A hot kadhai will not scar it, and it hides knife marks that a white stone shows.

04

Elevation & carving

Sandstone and carved panels for the outside face. We also handle the polishing and the fitting, so one team owns the finish.

Before you come

What people ask at the counter

How is marble priced?

By square foot, and the rate depends on the stone and the slab size, not just the name. Send the room size and we quote the actual slabs available, so the number you hear is the number you pay.

Marble or granite for the kitchen?

Granite, in almost every case. It takes a hot vessel without marking. Marble is for the drawing room, the stairs and the bathroom, where looks matter more than heat.

Do you do polishing and fitting?

Yes, our own team. You are not left to find a fitter after buying the slab.

Do you deliver outside Jalandhar?

Yes, across Punjab. Delivery is quoted by distance and slab weight.
